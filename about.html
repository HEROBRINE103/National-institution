About National Institution for Professional Studies

National Institution for Professional Studies (NIPS) is a premium English training institute focused on developing confidence, clarity, and global communication skills.

Located in a student-friendly learning environment, NIPS offers structured programs in English Speaking, IELTS, and TOEFL with result-oriented training methods.

Our Vision
